Some of My Favorite Locations Around Duluth

Created by Alex Marty

In this map, I've added some of my favorite places to go in the city of Duluth, Minnesota. A preexisting point is located at UMD where I go to major in GIS. I've also added Lester Park and Hartley Nature Center which are great places to go to get out in nautre in such a busy urban environemnt. I also love to go to the Aerial Lift Bridge in Canal Park to watch boats arrive to Duluth. I commonly go with my friend and keep a close eye on the ship tracker. These are all fairly close to where i live and I can take a quick drive to them or use the bus.
