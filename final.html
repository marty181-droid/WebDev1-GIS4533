Duluth Attractions

Final Project by Alex Marty

Welcome to my map! Here, you will find a map of the city of Duluth with several points scattered all over in places that are worth visiting while you're here. It includes activities like walking in nature, shopping, and seeing historical places that have shaped Duluth. Green pins represent nature areas, red pins are for structures, yellow pins are for historic places, and blue pins are for shopping and food!
